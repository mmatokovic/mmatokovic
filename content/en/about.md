## Hi, I'm Mislav Matoković

I'm a software developer from Croatia. By day I build software; in my free time I read and write about the things that fascinate me — history, prehistoric art and how science pieced together the story of our planet and of ourselves.

*Misli* means "thoughts" in Croatian, and that is what this blog is: a place where I write things down when I have something worth saying. You'll find posts here in both Croatian and English.
